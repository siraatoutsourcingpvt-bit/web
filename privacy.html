Zero-Data Architecture Summary: DeskFlow is designed to operate entirely on your device. The app does not require account registration and does not collect, transmit, or share personal information with the developer or third parties.

1. Introduction

DeskFlow is an offline agile project and sprint execution workspace application developed by SIRAAT OUTSOURCING (PVT.) LIMITED. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app. The application is designed to operate entirely on your device without collecting or transmitting personal information.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

DeskFlow does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal or sensitive data. This includes:

  • Names, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, Advertising ID)
  • IP addresses or network information
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Payment or financial information
  • Project workspaces, task descriptions, story points, roadmaps, or notes entered into the app

The app does not require account registration or user identification.

3. Local Processing

All agile boards, kanban lanes, sprint burndown calculations, and velocity metrics are processed locally on your device. The app does not send information to external servers.

4. Local Data Storage

Information you enter in the app, such as projects, tasks, story points, checklists, milestones, and settings, is stored locally on your device to provide app functionality. This information remains on your device and is not accessible to the developer.

5. Permissions

DeskFlow does not request sensitive Android permissions. The app does not require:

  • Internet access
  • Location access
  • Camera access
  • Microphone access
  • Contacts access
  • External storage access

All features operate completely offline.

6. Third-Party Services

DeskFlow does not use:

  • Advertising networks
  • Analytics services
  • Tracking SDKs
  • Cloud services
  • External data processors

The app functions independently without third-party integrations.

7. Children's Privacy

DeskFlow is suitable for users of all ages. Because the app does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect data from children or adults under the age of 13.

8. Data Security

All information remains on your device. The app does not transmit data externally. Data security depends on the security of your device. We recommend keeping your device updated and protected.

9. International Data Transfers

DeskFlow does not transfer data outside your device. No international data transfers occur.

10. User Control

You have full control over your data:

  • You may edit or delete projects, tasks, and checklists within the app
  • You may archive completed tasks or clear boards
  • You may clear app data through Android settings
  • You may uninstall the app at any time
  • Uninstalling removes all locally stored data

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if app functionality changes. Updates will be reflected by revising the “Last Updated” date above.
